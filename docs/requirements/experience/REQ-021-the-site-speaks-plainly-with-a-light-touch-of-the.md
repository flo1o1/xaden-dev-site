# The site speaks plainly, with a light touch of the Empyrean world

## Quality goal

Every page is understood on first reading by a developer whose first language is not English, and the voice carries the spirit of the Empyrean novels (a hard school, a narrow path, a code that holds) in small touches, never at the cost of clarity or truth.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given any sentence, then it is active, concrete and short, with no marketing filler ("simply", "just", "seamless", "revolutionary") and no promise that Xaden makes software safe or correct
- Given the world of the novels, then it appears only as names and short images (a section's eyebrow, the headline, the 404 page), at most one image per page, and always next to a plain statement of what is meant
- Given a term that comes from the novels or from Xaden (the parapet, the Code, the Trial, the Ledger, the Watch), then the page says in plain words what it stands for the first time it is used
- Given any heading in the docs, then it names the task or the answer it gives (REQ-002)
- Given a claim about what Xaden does, then it matches the released behavior or the page says it describes an unreleased preview
