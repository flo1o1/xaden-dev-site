# Visitors learn who is behind Xaden and how to reach them

## Job

When I want to know whether I can trust Xaden, I want to read who makes it, why, under which license and how to contact them, so I can judge the project and ask a question.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given the About page, then it states who, why, the MIT license and the contact email hello@xaden.dev
- Given the site footer, then it links to the contact email on every page
